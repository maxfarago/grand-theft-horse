Technical inspection:
- GLB 2.0; 181,808 bytes; 796 vertices; 984 triangles; 1 mesh; no textures or external dependencies.
- Vertex colors store a brown coat, dark mane/tail/hooves and eyes. Retain vertex colors.
- Forward is +Z; up is +Y. For travel toward -Z rotate the parent by PI around Y.
- Base bounds: min [-28.5, 0.8, -187.4], max [32.6, 183.1, 134.9].
- Union of all 15 morph targets: min [-33.2, -4.2, -192.4], max [34.6, 207.8, 134.9].
- Scale 0.01 gives full animated bounds approximately 0.678m wide, 2.12m high, 3.273m long.
- Root y=0.042 at scale 0.01 makes all gallop phases clear ground y=0.
- Single clip horse_A_, 1.5 seconds; 15 morph target frames and a repeated closing frame; 0.1 second keys.
- Loop at 1.0 second using AnimationMixer.clipAction(gltf.animations[0]).setDuration(1).play(), matching official Three.js example.
- Gallop vertical movement is built into geometry; extra body bouncing is optional and may exaggerate motion.
- Geometry has POSITION, COLOR_0, TEXCOORD_0 and an index, but no NORMAL attribute or morph normals. Original appearance is faceted. computeVertexNormals gives base smooth normals; proper moving smooth normals require matching normal deltas for every position morph target.
